2. Purpose and audience
SchoolAsist helps schools and education centers manage daily academics, attendance, results, family communication, tuition records and school transport in one place. Users include institution administrators, teachers, administrative/accounting/cafeteria staff, parents and students. Parents and students access their own institution-linked information; the app is not limited to employees of one business.

4. Services and integrations
Our HTTPS backend at maydanozasist.schoolasist.com provides authentication and institution-scoped data, with PostgreSQL storage. Notifications use Apple APNs/Firebase Cloud Messaging. Bus maps use OpenStreetMap tiles. Optional meetings use our Jitsi service at meet.schoolasist.com. The backend also supports configurable document recognition (Azure Document Intelligence), a local Ollama intent assistant, and institution-configured finance integrations; these are not needed for the demonstrated core flow. Tuition screens are institutional ledger records; the recording makes no real payment and shows no purchase of digital content.

5. Regional behavior
Feature access depends on institution and user role rather than country. The app is designed for Turkish institutions with Turkish as the default language and an English UI option. Educational content and institutional records depend on the institution, not a regional content catalogue.

6. Content and authorization
SchoolAsist is education-management software. Lesson documents, announcements and private messages are institution/user supplied; there is no public anonymous social feed. The developer confirms necessary rights and permissions for third-party material used in the app. OpenStreetMap attribution is provided in the map view. Review data is synthetic.
